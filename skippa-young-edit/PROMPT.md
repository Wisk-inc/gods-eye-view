# Master prompt — 3D typography lyric edit, "Young" (Skippa)

Paste the block below into a motion-design brief, an AI video/animation tool, or hand it to an editor. It
describes the whole edit: format, timing, type, every asset, every effect, and a shot-by-shot script.
Times are for the song's tempo (**116 BPM**); beat numbers let you move it to any other clip.

---

## THE PROMPT

Create a **21.5-second 3D kinetic-typography lyric edit**, 1920×1080, 30 fps, in the style of a high-end
After Effects + Element 3D "3D typography edit": **one word (or short phrase) on screen at a time, each word a
heavy extruded 3D object in its own miniature set, a virtual camera whipping from word to word, every beat
punctuated by a flash, a wipe or a punch-zoom.** Dense, glowing, saturated, never still.

### 1. Story and tone
The song is a young man looking back at himself: young but worn out by sleepless nights, alone with his
thoughts, fighting himself, facing his demons, and wondering how people will speak of him once he is gone. The
edit must *show* that arc, not just print words:
- **Act 1 — heat (0–5.2 s):** red, loud, liquid. Youth as pressure. Words slam, burn white-hot, roll.
- **Act 2 — alone (2.6–3.9 s inside act 1):** the frame opens through an iris onto deep space: a huge blue
  planet, a rising sun, and one tiny hooded figure standing on the planet's edge. The word is gold.
- **Act 3 — the fight (3.9–7.9 s):** maroon and crimson, cards and a spiky sunburst spinning around the words;
  on the biggest word the **colour drains out of the picture** — night taking over.
- **Act 4 — the fall (8.7–10.4 s, no words):** black-and-white. A thin band of light opens like an eyelid; a
  white silhouette of a young man falls slowly through outline stars; the image strobes negative.
- **Act 5 — the reckoning (10.4–12.0 s):** one three-word line stretches across the whole frame in cold chrome
  under a cracked moon, bracket lines closing in on it like a vice.
- **Act 6 — when I'm gone (12.0–17.2 s):** steel-grey words floating in a vast dark space full of drifting
  strings of light, black tiles and a single small cyan flare that follows the words like a living thing. Real
  depth of field: each word blurs as the next arrives. It ends on the title word, held, perfectly lit.
- **Outro (17.2–20.2 s):** smoke wipes to purple; a ball of particles condenses into an engraved roundel
  (crown over a "Y", ring text "YOUNG · SKIPPA"); it catches a sweep of light, bursts, and the frame tunnels
  into a glowing call-to-action that glitches out to black.

### 2. Timing
- Tempo **116 BPM**: quarter note 0.517 s (15.5 frames), 8th 0.259 s, 16th 0.129 s. Beat 0 at **0.116 s**.
- The clip is **38.9 beats** of picture, then 1.3 s of black.
- **Words enter on the onset of the sung word** (never early). **Flashes, wipes and scene changes sit on
  quarter beats or 8ths.** Letters inside a word arrive **1–2 frames apart** with overshoot.
- Centre every transition on its beat: start the smear 3–5 frames early so the new scene is readable *on* the
  beat.
- Add a small punch on every quarter beat: 2–3 % zoom kick, +10–20 % exposure, extra chromatic aberration,
  decaying over 4–6 frames.

### 3. Lettering
- **Face:** the typeface is **Chinese Rocks** (Typodermic), slanted 0.19 to the right — ultra-heavy condensed
  squared display capitals, each letter about **1 : 2** (width : height).
  Rectangular bowls with chamfered corners; counters are narrow vertical slots a third of the letter wide;
  small spurs on stroke ends. **Rough-cut edges**: slightly non-parallel sides, fine tooth, scattered chips —
  as if cut by hand from sheet metal. Letters vary a little in height, baseline and rotation.
- **3D:** extrude 30–60 % of cap height, thin bright bevel. Always turn the word 15–25° in yaw and 10–20° in
  pitch so the sides show (usually toward lower-left). Never perfectly level: roll between −15° and +8°.
- **Materials:** (red acts) salmon-to-white face gradient, pure white bevel edges, saturated red sides falling to
  near-black at the back, red halo behind; (crimson) dark red faces, lighter tops; (gold) warm gold faces with
  glowing yellow bevels and dark brown sides; (mono / steel) brushed silver faces with a soft horizontal
  reflection band, graphite sides. Faint surface wear on every face.
- **Scale:** cap height 22–45 % of frame height. One giant word at ~70 % (overflowing). The long word ~12 %.
- **Entrances:** drop from above / rise from below / alternate both / tumble in from a corner one letter at a
  time / slam from the lens (starts enormous) / swing in from lower-right. **Exits:** whip, tip backward and
  darken, roll away with the camera, defocus, or vanish under a flash.

### 4. Asset kit (all flat "shape layers" floating in 3D at different depths)
1. **Tapered white strokes** with pointed ends and a trace of grey shading, animated by trim path (draw on in
   3–4 frames, hold, erase): thin arcs and loops around words, wavy S-curves across them, straight slashes,
   thick crescents shaped like `(` and `)`, a thick ring, two giant pillars, two beams from below.
2. **Red rounded cards** with thin cream outlines, tilted, some arranged radially like petals, one directly
   behind a word as a label plate. **Black tiles** with a hairline light edge. One **wireframe tile**.
3. **Bokeh rings** — white ring, soft halo, dark centre — 12–20 per shot from tiny to large, drifting upward.
4. **Dotted light strings** — chains of soft white dots along long curving paths, near and far.
5. **Spiky sunburst** — red spiky ring, cream outline, long thin cream rays, dark spoked disc, magenta ring arc.
6. **Backgrounds:** liquid red/white ink with a blue-white burst; dark maroon concentric ripples; a fan of red
   polygon panels with black gaps; maroon with big magenta planes; black; teal-grey gradient; deep purple.
7. **Scenery:** blue planet with cyan atmosphere rim and star specks; sun with lens flare; cracked grey moon;
   tiny hooded standing figure; white falling-figure silhouette; two bevelled outline stars; thick `<` `>`
   chevrons; two three-segment bracket lines; a few tiny 3D music notes.
8. **Light:** anamorphic lens flare (blue, later cyan) with hexagon ghosts; warm light leaks from corners; glints.
9. **Atmosphere:** drifting dust/embers in every shot; smoke for the wipe and the outro.
10. **Overlays:** letterbox bars that breathe per shot (0 → 4–9 % each; 19–39 % in the interlude; partly
    transparent); a small outlined credit line bottom-centre at ~40 % opacity; film grain everywhere.

### 5. Finishing stack (on every frame)
Heavy motion blur (180–290° shutter) · bloom on whites and flares · chromatic aberration rising toward the
edges · vignette · slight sharpening · grain. Section-specific: turbulent displacement and film scratches (ink
act), radial zoom blur, iris wipes, colour drain, polarity inversion, mirrored edges, slice and block glitches,
RGB split, an edge-detect frame, smoke wipe.

### 7. Words
26 slots, filled in order. The delivered file uses original lines:
`YOUNG / BUT / THE / NIGHTS / FEEL / OLD / ALONE / I FOUGHT / WITH / MY / MYSELF / EVERY / NIGHT / NO / SLEEP /
FACING MY DEMONS / WHEN / I'M GONE / WILL / THEY / SAY / THAT / I / I WAS / ONLY / YOUNG`.
To use the song's own lyrics, replace them with the lines sung in the clip window (49 words; words per slot
`1 1 1 2 1 3 3 3 2 2 2 1 3 4 2 | 2 1 3 2 1 1 1 1 1 1 4`).

### 8. Rules
- Every frame has at least five layers. No plain word-on-gradient shots.
- Nothing holds still: slow push or drift on every hold, rings and dust always moving.
- No clean cuts: hide every scene change in blur, a flash, an iris or matching motion.
- All imagery original: no footage, no existing logos, no recognisable characters.
- Keep the credit line and the call-to-action configurable.
